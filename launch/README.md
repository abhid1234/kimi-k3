## The angle

The hook is **not** "I built a planning tool." It's the scoring bug: a plan-quality meter
that returned 100/100 for every plan, which made compare mode's winner badge meaningless.
That's specific, it's true, it happened during this build, and it generalises to a real
claim about AI products shipping confidence theater.

The tool is the evidence. Lead with the bug.
